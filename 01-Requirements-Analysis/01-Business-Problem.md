# Business Problem

## Background

Cape Learning Institute (CLI) is a fictional tertiary education institution that provides academic programmes and student support services. Its Student Support Department assists students with a range of enquiries, including academic support, registration, financial queries, course information, technical issues and general assistance.

## Current Process

Students currently submit support requests through multiple channels, including email, WhatsApp, telephone calls and in-person visits. Support staff manually record and manage these requests using spreadsheets and email.

As the number of students has increased, managing requests across multiple channels has become increasingly difficult.

## Business Problem

The Student Support Department does not have a centralised and consistent way to capture, assign, track and monitor student support requests.

This results in requests being missed or overlooked, duplicate requests being created, uncertainty about who is responsible for individual requests, delays in responding to students and inconsistent information being recorded.

Managers also have limited visibility of the status and performance of support requests and must spend additional time manually compiling information for reporting.

## Impact on the Business

The current process can result in:

- Delays in responding to student requests.
- Requests being missed or overlooked.
- Duplicate requests being created across different communication channels.
- Difficulty assigning requests to the appropriate staff member.
- Inconsistent or incomplete information being recorded.
- Difficulty identifying overdue requests.
- Limited management visibility of support activity and performance.
- Additional administrative work for support staff and managers.

## Problem Statement

CLI's current student support request process relies on multiple communication channels and manual record-keeping methods, making it difficult to consistently capture, assign, track and monitor student requests. This reduces the department's ability to respond efficiently, maintain accurate records and provide management with reliable visibility of support activities.
