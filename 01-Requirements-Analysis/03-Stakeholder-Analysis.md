# Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose

Stakeholder analysis is used to identify the people or groups affected by the student support process, understand their needs and concerns, and determine how they may influence or be affected by the proposed improvement.

For this fictional case study, stakeholder needs are based on the defined business problem and simulated stakeholder perspectives. No real stakeholder interviews were conducted.

## Stakeholder Groups

| Stakeholder | Role / Interest | Influence | Key Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Students | Submit support requests and require assistance | Medium | Easy request submission, clear status updates, timely responses and reliable support |
| Support Staff | Receive, manage, assign and resolve support requests | High | Complete request information, clear responsibilities, prioritisation and easy tracking |
| Support Manager | Oversees the Student Support Department | High | Visibility of workload, overdue requests, performance and reporting |
| IT / System Administrator | Responsible for supporting and maintaining the system | High | Secure system, reliable performance, user management and maintainability |
| Academic / Faculty Staff | May assist with academic-related student requests | Medium | Relevant request information, clear assignments and communication |
| Senior Management | Interested in service performance and business outcomes | High | Reliable reports, performance information and evidence of improvement |

## Stakeholder Needs

### Students

Students need a simple and consistent way to submit support requests. They should be able to provide the necessary information and receive updates about the progress of their requests.

### Support Staff

Support staff need a centralised view of requests so that they can see what needs to be handled, who is responsible for each request and which requests require attention.

### Support Manager

The Support Manager needs visibility of the overall support process. This includes the number of requests received, outstanding requests, overdue requests, response times and workload distribution.

### IT / System Administrator

The IT or System Administrator needs the system to be secure, reliable and manageable. They may also be responsible for user access, technical maintenance and system support.

### Academic / Faculty Staff

Academic or faculty staff may need access to requests that require academic assistance. They need sufficient information to understand the issue and respond appropriately.

### Senior Management

Senior Management needs accurate information about the performance of the Student Support Department to support monitoring and decision-making.

## Stakeholder Influence and Interest

| Stakeholder | Level of Interest | Level of Influence | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Students | High | Medium | Surveys, interviews and feedback |
| Support Staff | High | High | Interviews, workshops and observation |
| Support Manager | High | High | Interviews and requirements workshops |
| IT / System Administrator | Medium | High | Technical discussions and reviews |
| Academic / Faculty Staff | Medium | Medium | Interviews and workshops |
| Senior Management | Medium | High | Management discussions and reporting reviews |

## Key Stakeholder Considerations

The analysis shows that different stakeholders have different priorities. Students are primarily concerned with accessibility, communication and timely support, while support staff require efficient tools for managing their workload.

Management is more focused on visibility, reporting and performance, while IT stakeholders are concerned with security, reliability and maintainability.

These differences will need to be considered when analysing and prioritising the requirements.
