# Requirements Elicitation Plan

## Purpose

The purpose of the requirements elicitation process is to gather sufficient information about the needs, problems and expectations of the stakeholders involved in the student support process.

As this is a fictional portfolio case study, the elicitation activities below represent a simulated Business Analysis approach. No real stakeholder interviews or observations were conducted.

## Elicitation Objectives

The elicitation process aims to:

- Understand how student support requests are currently submitted and managed.
- Identify problems and inefficiencies in the current process.
- Understand the needs of students and support staff.
- Identify the information that needs to be captured for each request.
- Understand how requests should be assigned and prioritised.
- Determine how stakeholders need to track request progress.
- Identify reporting and monitoring requirements.
- Identify potential constraints, risks and dependencies.
- Provide sufficient information to define clear and testable requirements.

## Elicitation Techniques

| Technique | Stakeholders | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Interviews | Support Staff, Support Manager, Students | Gather detailed information about experiences, problems and needs |
| Questionnaires / Surveys | Students | Gather feedback from a larger number of users about their support experience |
| Observation | Support Staff | Understand how requests are currently received, recorded and managed |
| Document Analysis | Support Manager, Support Staff | Review existing spreadsheets, request records, procedures and reports |
| Workshops | Support Staff, Support Manager, IT / System Administrator | Discuss requirements, identify issues and reach agreement |
| Existing-System Analysis | IT / System Administrator, Support Staff | Understand current tools, limitations and technical dependencies |

## Interview Approach

Semi-structured interviews would be used with key stakeholders. A consistent set of questions would be prepared while allowing additional questions to be asked when clarification is required.

Questions would focus on:

- Current processes.
- Common problems.
- Information currently captured.
- User needs.
- Priorities.
- Reporting requirements.
- Security and access needs.
- Desired improvements.

## Observation Approach

Support staff would be observed while handling student support requests.

The observation would focus on:

- How requests are received.
- How information is recorded.
- How requests are assigned.
- How staff determine priorities.
- How request status is tracked.
- How completed requests are recorded.
- Where delays or duplicate work occur.

## Document Analysis

Existing documents and records would be reviewed to understand the current process and information requirements.

Potential documents include:

- Support request spreadsheets.
- Email records.
- Existing procedures.
- Request categories.
- Staff allocation records.
- Management reports.
- Service-level guidelines, if available.

## Workshop Approach

A requirements workshop would bring relevant stakeholders together to discuss the current problems and agree on the most important requirements.

The workshop would aim to:

1. Confirm the business problem.
2. Review stakeholder needs.
3. Identify key requirements.
4. Discuss conflicting needs or priorities.
5. Identify constraints and dependencies.
6. Prioritise important requirements.
7. Confirm the expected business outcome.

## Elicitation Considerations

The Business Analyst should avoid making assumptions about stakeholder needs without evidence. Information gathered from different stakeholders should be compared and validated to identify inconsistencies, missing information or conflicting requirements.

Because this is a simulated case study, any stakeholder findings used in later sections will be clearly identified as simulated findings or assumptions rather than real-world research.

## Expected Outcome

The elicitation process should provide enough information to analyse, document and prioritise the requirements for improving CLI's student support request process.

The findings will be used to develop the requirements documentation and acceptance criteria for the proposed solution.
