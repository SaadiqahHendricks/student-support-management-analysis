# Stakeholder Needs and Bottlenecks

## Purpose

This document identifies the key needs of stakeholders involved in the student support process and analyses the bottlenecks and pain points within the current As-Is process.

The analysis is based on the fictional Cape Learning Institute (CLI) case study and simulated stakeholder findings.

## Stakeholder Needs

### Students

Students need:

- A simple and consistent way to submit support requests.
- Confirmation that their request has been received.
- Clear information about the status of their request.
- Updates when additional information is required.
- Timely responses to their requests.
- A reliable way to communicate with the Student Support Department.

### Support Staff

Support staff need:

- A centralised view of student support requests.
- Consistent information for each request.
- Clear responsibility for each request.
- The ability to prioritise requests.
- An easy way to identify outstanding and overdue requests.
- Access to sufficient information to resolve requests.
- A reliable way to update request statuses.
- Reduced manual administration and duplicate data entry.

### Support Manager

The Support Manager needs:

- Visibility of all active and outstanding requests.
- Information about requests assigned to each staff member.
- The ability to identify overdue requests.
- Information about response and resolution times.
- Reliable information for management reporting.
- Visibility of workload and request volumes.
- Information that can support decision-making and process improvement.

### Academic / Faculty Staff

Academic and faculty staff need:

- Relevant information when a request is referred to them.
- Clear responsibility for requests requiring academic assistance.
- A reliable way to receive referred requests.
- Visibility of the information required to resolve the student's issue.
- Clear communication between departments.

### IT / System Administrator

The IT or System Administrator needs:

- Controlled access to student information.
- Clearly defined user roles and permissions.
- A reliable system for managing support information.
- Secure handling of sensitive student information.
- A solution that can be maintained and supported effectively.

## Stakeholder Needs Summary

| Stakeholder | Main Need | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Students | Easy request submission and status visibility | To improve the support experience |
| Support Staff | Centralised request management | To manage requests efficiently |
| Support Manager | Reporting and workload visibility | To monitor performance and make decisions |
| Academic / Faculty Staff | Clear referral and request information | To resolve academic-related requests efficiently |
| IT / System Administrator | Security and controlled access | To protect information and maintain the solution |

## Bottlenecks and Pain Points

A **bottleneck** is a point in a process where work becomes delayed, restricted or overloaded.

A **pain point** is a problem or difficulty experienced by people involved in a process.

The following bottlenecks and pain points have been identified in the current student support process.

### 1. Multiple Communication Channels

Students can submit requests through email, WhatsApp, telephone and in-person visits.

**Impact:**

- Requests may be spread across different channels.
- Staff may have difficulty maintaining a consistent record.
- Information can be overlooked.
- Duplicate requests may be created.

**Process Area Affected:** Request submission and recording.

### 2. Manual Data Entry

Support staff manually record requests in spreadsheets after receiving them.

**Impact:**

- Additional administrative work is created.
- Information may be entered incorrectly.
- Staff must spend time maintaining records.
- Duplicate data entry may occur.

**Process Area Affected:** Request recording.

### 3. Unclear Request Responsibility

Requests need to be manually assigned to the appropriate staff member.

**Impact:**

- It may not always be clear who is responsible for a request.
- Requests may take longer to reach the correct person.
- Staff workload may become uneven.
- Requests may be overlooked.

**Process Area Affected:** Request assignment.

### 4. Manual Status Tracking

Request statuses are manually updated in spreadsheets.

**Impact:**

- Status information may become outdated.
- Staff may have difficulty identifying the latest position of a request.
- Managers have limited real-time visibility.
- Students may not know the progress of their request.

**Process Area Affected:** Request tracking.

### 5. Difficulty Identifying Overdue Requests

The current process does not provide an efficient way to identify requests that have exceeded the expected response timeframe.

**Impact:**

- Delayed requests may remain unnoticed.
- Staff may not know which requests require urgent attention.
- Management has limited visibility of service delays.

**Process Area Affected:** Request monitoring.

### 6. Referral Between Departments

Some requests require assistance from another department or staff member.

**Impact:**

- Information may need to be transferred manually.
- Responsibility may become unclear during the handoff.
- Additional delays may occur.
- The original request may be difficult to track during the referral.

**Process Area Affected:** Request handling and referral.

### 7. Manual Reporting

Management information must be manually compiled from support records.

**Impact:**

- Reporting takes additional time.
- Information may require manual checking.
- Reports may not provide an immediate view of current activity.
- Management may have difficulty identifying trends.

**Process Area Affected:** Reporting and monitoring.

## Bottleneck Summary

| Bottleneck / Pain Point | Cause | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Multiple communication channels | Requests arrive through different channels | Requests may be missed or duplicated |
| Manual data entry | Requests are manually recorded | Increased administration and data-entry errors |
| Unclear responsibility | Requests are manually assigned | Delays and unclear ownership |
| Manual status tracking | Status is updated manually | Limited visibility of progress |
| Overdue requests | No efficient overdue monitoring | Delayed requests may go unnoticed |
| Department referrals | Requests are handed between staff or departments | Possible delays and unclear responsibility |
| Manual reporting | Information must be manually compiled | Increased reporting effort |

## Process Improvement Opportunities

The identified bottlenecks indicate several areas where the student support process could be improved.

Potential improvement areas include:

- Centralising student support requests.
- Standardising the information captured for each request.
- Assigning clear ownership to requests.
- Introducing defined request statuses.
- Supporting request prioritisation.
- Improving visibility of overdue requests.
- Improving the tracking of referred requests.
- Providing structured information for management reporting.
- Reducing unnecessary manual administration.

These improvement opportunities will be considered when designing the future-state To-Be process.

## Case Study Note

This is a fictional Business Analysis portfolio case study. The stakeholder needs, bottlenecks and pain points represent simulated findings based on the defined business problem and process analysis.

No real stakeholder interviews or observations were conducted.
