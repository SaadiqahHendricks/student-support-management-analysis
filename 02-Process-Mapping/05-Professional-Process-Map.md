# Professional Process Map

## Purpose

This document presents the professional process mapping analysis for the Cape Learning Institute (CLI) Student Support Management System case study.

The process maps provide a clear representation of the current-state (As-Is) and proposed future-state (To-Be) student support processes.

The analysis is based on the requirements, stakeholder needs, bottlenecks, inputs and outputs identified in the previous Business Analysis documents.

## Process Mapping Standards

The process maps have been designed to be:

- Clear and easy to understand.
- Logically structured.
- Consistent in their presentation.
- Focused on the student support request process.
- Connected to the identified business problems.
- Suitable for comparing the current and proposed future states.
- Detailed enough to demonstrate the movement of a request through the process.

# As-Is Process Map

## Current-State Process

The following table represents the current-state student support process from the beginning of a request to its resolution.

| Step | Process Activity | Responsible Actor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Student identifies a problem or requires assistance | Student |
| 2 | Student contacts Student Support | Student |
| 3 | Request is received through email, WhatsApp, telephone or in-person contact | Student / Support Staff |
| 4 | Support staff reviews the request | Support Staff |
| 5 | Determine whether sufficient information is available | Support Staff |
| 6 | If information is missing, contact the student for additional information | Support Staff |
| 7 | Student provides the required information | Student |
| 8 | Support staff reviews the request again | Support Staff |
| 9 | Request is recorded in a spreadsheet | Support Staff |
| 10 | Request is reviewed and categorised | Support Staff |
| 11 | Request is assigned to an appropriate staff member | Support Staff |
| 12 | Assigned staff member investigates and handles the request | Support Staff |
| 13 | Determine whether additional assistance is required | Support Staff |
| 14 | If required, request is referred to the appropriate department | Support Staff / Academic or Faculty Staff |
| 15 | Additional information or assistance is received | Academic / Faculty Staff |
| 16 | Staff member continues handling the request | Support Staff |
| 17 | Student receives a response | Support Staff |
| 18 | Request information is manually updated in the spreadsheet | Support Staff |
| 19 | Request is considered resolved | Support Staff |
| 20 | Process ends | Support Staff |

## As-Is Decision Points

The current-state process contains two main decision points:

### Decision 1: Is sufficient information available?

If sufficient information is not available, support staff must contact the student and request additional information before continuing with the request.

If sufficient information is available, the request can continue to recording and processing.

### Decision 2: Is additional assistance required?

If additional assistance is required, the request is referred to the appropriate department or staff member.

If additional assistance is not required, the assigned staff member continues with the request until it can be resolved.

## As-Is Process Problems

The current-state process demonstrates several areas where inefficiencies may occur:

- Requests are received through multiple communication channels.
- Request information may be incomplete when initially received.
- Requests are manually recorded in spreadsheets.
- Request ownership may not always be immediately clear.
- Request status is manually maintained.
- Overdue requests may be difficult to identify.
- Referrals to other departments may require manual follow-up.
- Management reporting requires information to be compiled manually.
- Duplicate requests may occur when students use multiple communication channels.

# To-Be Process Map

## Future-State Process

The following table represents the proposed future-state student support process.

| Step | Process Activity | Responsible Actor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Student identifies a problem or requires assistance | Student |
| 2 | Student submits a support request through the centralised system | Student |
| 3 | System captures and records the request | System |
| 4 | System generates a unique request reference number | System |
| 5 | Student receives confirmation of submission | System / Student |
| 6 | Support staff reviews the submitted request | Support Staff |
| 7 | Determine whether sufficient information is available | Support Staff |
| 8 | If information is missing, request additional information from the student | Support Staff |
| 9 | Student provides the required information | Student |
| 10 | Request record is updated | System / Support Staff |
| 11 | Request is reviewed again | Support Staff |
| 12 | Request is categorised | Support Staff |
| 13 | Request is prioritised according to urgency and impact | Support Staff |
| 14 | Request is assigned to the appropriate staff member | Support Staff |
| 15 | Request status is updated to "Assigned" or "In Progress" | Support Staff / System |
| 16 | Assigned staff member investigates and handles the request | Support Staff |
| 17 | Determine whether additional assistance is required | Support Staff |
| 18 | If required, request is referred to the appropriate department | Support Staff |
| 19 | Referral is recorded and tracked | System / Support Staff |
| 20 | Required information or assistance is received | Academic / Faculty Staff |
| 21 | Request handling continues | Support Staff |
| 22 | Request is resolved | Support Staff |
| 23 | Student receives a response regarding the outcome | Support Staff |
| 24 | Request outcome and final information are recorded | Support Staff / System |
| 25 | Request is closed | Support Staff |
| 26 | Management information is updated for monitoring and reporting | System / Support Manager |
| 27 | Process ends | System / Support Staff |

## To-Be Decision Points

The future-state process contains two main decision points:

### Decision 1: Is sufficient information available?

If information is missing, the student is asked to provide the required information.

Once the information is received, the request record is updated and the request is reviewed again.

If sufficient information is already available, the request proceeds to categorisation and prioritisation.

### Decision 2: Is additional assistance required?

If additional assistance is required, the request is referred to the appropriate department.

The referral is recorded and tracked before the request continues.

If no additional assistance is required, the staff member continues handling the request until it is resolved.

# As-Is vs To-Be Comparison

| Process Area | As-Is Process | To-Be Process | Expected Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Request submission | Multiple communication channels | Centralised system | More consistent request capture |
| Request recording | Manual spreadsheet entry | Structured digital capture | Reduced manual administration |
| Request identification | No standard reference number | Unique request reference number | Easier request tracking |
| Request information | May be incomplete or inconsistent | Standardised information | Improved data quality |
| Categorisation | Manually reviewed | Standardised categories | More consistent classification |
| Prioritisation | Limited visibility | Requests can be prioritised | Better workload management |
| Assignment | Manually assigned | Clear ownership recorded | Improved accountability |
| Status tracking | Manually maintained | Standardised statuses | Better visibility |
| Referrals | Manual referral and follow-up | Referral recorded and tracked | Improved handover |
| Overdue requests | Difficult to identify | Requests can be monitored | Improved response management |
| Student communication | Multiple communication channels | Structured communication | Better visibility |
| Reporting | Manually compiled | Structured management information | More efficient reporting |

---

# Process Improvement Summary

The To-Be process addresses the main problems identified during the As-Is process analysis.

The most significant improvement is the introduction of a centralised process for capturing and managing student support requests.

The proposed process also introduces:

- Standardised request information.
- Unique request reference numbers.
- Request categorisation.
- Request prioritisation.
- Clear request ownership.
- Standardised request statuses.
- Request tracking.
- Referral tracking.
- Improved overdue request monitoring.
- Structured management information.

These improvements are intended to reduce manual administration, improve request visibility and make it easier for support staff and management to monitor requests throughout their lifecycle.

# Process Mapping Conclusion

The professional process maps demonstrate the transition from the current As-Is process to the proposed To-Be process.

The As-Is process highlights the manual activities, multiple communication channels and limited visibility that contribute to the identified business problems.

The To-Be process introduces a more structured approach that centralises request management, improves ownership and tracking, and provides better information for students, support staff and management.

The process maps demonstrate how Business Analysis can be used to understand an existing process, identify areas for improvement and design a future-state process that is aligned with business requirements.
